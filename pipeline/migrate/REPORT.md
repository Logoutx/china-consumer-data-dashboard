## Addendum (2026-07-08): item 6's ordering claim reversed

Item 6 above asserted that sorting the annual "2016"-style row *after* "2016-Q1/Q2/Q3" was
the chronologically-correct choice, and `period_sort_key` implemented that (annual at
month-rank 13, i.e. after every quarter). That reasoning conflated **when NBS published**
the annual print (in December, after Q3) with **what period it covers** (the full year,
